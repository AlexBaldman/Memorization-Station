# Google Translate Mining Strategy

Turn your translation history into a high-performance memory palace. This guide outlines how to extract your recent translations and transform them into mnemonic flashcards.

## Phase 1: Extraction

Currently, Google Translate doesn't offer a real-time neural link for history, but you can extract your data manually:

1.  **Google Takeout**: Go to [takeout.google.com](https://takeout.google.com) and select only "Google Translate". This will provide a CSV or JSON of your entire history.
2.  **Manual Clipboard Mining**: Open your Google Translate History page and copy the list of recent translations.

## Phase 2: Card Creation

Once you have your list of words, use the **Card Creator** to build permanence:

1.  **Open Card Creator**: Click "CREATE CARDS" on the home screen.
2.  **Paste & Sync**: Paste a word from your history into the **Portuguese Original** field.
3.  **Trigger Magic Assist**: Click the **Sparkle Icon** (Wand) next to the input.
4.  **Neural Scaffolding**: The AI will automatically detect the language, translate it, generate a vivid Michel Thomas/Metivier mnemonic story, and provide the correct conjugation matrix if it's a verb.
